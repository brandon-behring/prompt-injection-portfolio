## Thesis

Five methodology lessons emerge from the portfolio's 6 lanes + the
submission predecessor's evidence:

1. **Composition is fate** (Ch 2): train/test data composition determines
   achievable AUPRC ceilings. The OOD wall is data-bound at the LoRA
   parameter budget (per Round 15 Q1 framing) — Lane 2's outcome confirms
   or refutes.
2. **The rung ladder is an instrument** (Ch 4): pretraining does ~68%
   of the work (V0 finding); fine-tuning is the +/-22% delta. Frozen-
   probe baseline is the right floor.
3. **CIs replace p-values** (Ch 5): paired-bootstrap delta CIs + pre-
   registered stopping rules (V4 finding) protect against regression
   hiding. Single-class slices need val-fixed TPR (single-class metric
   convention enforced upstream per ADR-027 retirement).
4. **Threshold policy is where calibration breaks** (Ch 6): val→OOD
   transfer fails when distribution shifts. APR (per ADR-037) frames
   the utility-security tradeoff honestly.
5. **Encoder-only detection has a structural ceiling** (Chs 7-12 cumulative):
   neither character_injection bypass robustness (Lane 1b H∅), nor
   Spotlighting transfer (Lane 3 H∅), nor TaskTracker port (Lane 5 H∅)
   reliably crosses the wall at LoRA parameter budget. Score fusion
   (Lane 4) helps at the margin but doesn't change the floor.

CodeIntegrity's 98% post-mortem is the cautionary anchor: published
detector accuracy ≠ deployed detector robustness.
