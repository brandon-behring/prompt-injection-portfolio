## Thesis

TaskTracker (Abdelnabi et al. 2024 arXiv 2406.00799) extracts activation
deltas at a transformer layer and trains a linear classifier on those
deltas. The methodology was designed for decoder LMs where the prompt-vs-
completion boundary creates the activation signal. Lane 5 ports the
methodology to ModernBERT-base (encoder-only) — F8 risk anticipates the
port test will fail (encoder vs decoder methodology mismatch).

## R — Representation

`ActivationDeltaProbe` per eval-toolkit v0.43+ canonical surface;
`backbone="answerdotai/ModernBERT-base"`, `layer_index=-1`,
`aggregate="mean"`. Trains on Lane 2 MR-3 corpus per Round 16 Q4
cross-lane comparability.
