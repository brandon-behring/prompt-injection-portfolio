## Thesis

Spotlighting (Hines et al. 2024 arXiv 2403.14720) is a structural
preprocessing defense designed for decoder-LLM rejection. The encoder-
classifier port test asks: do the 3 variants (`DelimitVariant` +
`DatamarkVariant` + `EncodeVariant` via eval-toolkit v0.47) help, hurt,
or no-op when applied as preprocessing to encoder-only detectors?

## R — Representation

3 Spotlighting variants as dataclass strategies satisfying the
`TextTransform` Protocol per eval-toolkit v0.47 canonical surface.

## O — Operation

Top-level `sweep(strategies, texts, scorer=detector)` over 3 variants ×
N detectors. RAG demo (HF Space) makes the structural-defense concept
tangible for the L0 reader.

## E — Evolution

Hines et al. designed Spotlighting for LLM-judge usage; encoder port is
the open question. Per Round 7 Q1'''''' Lane 3 hypothesis: structural
defense → encoder transfer likely null-or-negative; Spotlighting's
benefit lives where the LLM does, not where the encoder does.
