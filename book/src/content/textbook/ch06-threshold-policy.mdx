## Thesis

Threshold policy is where submission's calibration broke; val-fixed TPR
transfer fails when OOD distribution shifts. The companion notebook lets
reader pick a target FPR + see the corresponding TPR + threshold + val→test
drift.

operating points; single-class slate convention (val-fixed TPR only on
single-class slices; AUPRC/AUROC `status="skipped"` per ADR-027 upstream-
enforced via eval-toolkit#39).

calibrator family (Platt + Beta + Isotonic + Temperature; per submission
ADR-055/056/058).

→ APR metric (per ADR-037; utility-aware threshold selection per
Meta PG2 framing).
