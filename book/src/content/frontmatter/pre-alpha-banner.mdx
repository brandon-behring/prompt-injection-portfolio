# Pre-Alpha Status

> **The OOD Wall is pre-alpha (v0.1.0-pre at time of writing).**

What pre-alpha means here:

- **ADRs are not yet locked**. They will lock at v0.7.0 (M7 ratification).
  Until then, methodology decisions may evolve as lane experiments close.
- **Freshness badges are visible** on every chapter + companion notebook.
  States progress: `exploratory` → `experimental-result` → `locked` →
  (occasionally) `superseded`. Pre-alpha = the entire repo is at one of
  the first two states.
- **Cost discipline applies**. Per plan §16: $250 base envelope + $100
  contingency. Tier C unlocks require a `decisions/contingency_unlock_N.md`
  entry citing the specific interim signal that justified the spend.
- **Build-in-public cadence is active**. Weekly progress threads + monthly
  deep-dives live at `docs/build-in-public/` and on Twitter/X + Mastodon.
- **Three guides ship sequentially per Round 17 Q3**:
  - **v0.7.0 (M7)**: textbook guide ratified. Pre-alpha banner removed on
    textbook routes (`/textbook/*`).
  - **v0.8.0 (~month 13)**: narrative guide ("Can we climb the wall?")
    shipped. Pre-alpha banner removed on narrative routes (`/narrative/*`).
  - **v0.9.0 (~month 14)**: academic IMRaD guide shipped. Pre-alpha banner
    removed on academic routes (`/academic/*`).
  - **v1.0.0 (~month 16-17)**: all three guides polished + citable.
    Pre-alpha lifecycle complete.

The portfolio's research finding (per submission predecessor's ADR-075):
**the OOD wall is real**, methodologically load-bearing, and the
governing constraint of detector evaluation in 2025-26. Portfolio's
question: is it data-bound or structural?

Pre-alpha banner is removed per-guide as the corresponding ship gate
passes. Until then, **read findings as in-progress observations + cite
specific ADRs / experiment-record SHAs rather than the live site URL**.
