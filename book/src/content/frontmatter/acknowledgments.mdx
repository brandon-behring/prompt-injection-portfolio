# Acknowledgments

This portfolio builds on the submission predecessor `prompt-injection-detection-prototype`
(<https://github.com/brandon-behring/prompt-injection-detection-prototype>) and
its 79 ADRs at v1.3.0. The submission's hub-and-spoke artifact structure
(replaced at v1.3.0 with two-guide reader architecture) was the prompt for
portfolio's Round 17 3-guide direction.

## Methodology heritage

- **OWASP LLM01:2025 (Prompt Injection)** — threat taxonomy framework.
- **MITRE ATLAS** — AML.T0051.000 + AML.T0051.001 mappings.
- **Greshake et al. 2023** ("Not What You've Signed Up For", arXiv 2302.12173)
  — canonical reference for indirect-injection threat.
- **Submission predecessor** — frozen-probe baseline + LODO methodology +
  source-disjoint splits + paired-bootstrap CI apparatus + dual-policy
  thresholds + single-class metric convention.
