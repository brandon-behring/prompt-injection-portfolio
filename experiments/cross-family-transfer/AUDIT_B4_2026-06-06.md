## The one correction (V4 — independently re-verified)

Claim §4(ii) — "the cross-family wall and the 38.5% over-defense are two faces of lexical-shortcut
overfitting" — is **over-stated**. At the same val-fixed threshold, **21.4%** of the genuine held-out
*test* negatives already fire (vs 38.5% NotInject), so **~56% of the over-defense is generic threshold
miscalibration under distribution shift**, not trigger-specific. The trigger-attributable excess is
**+17.2pp mean but seed-variable** (seed-0 +4.9, seed-1 +24.6, seed-2 +22.0). The "keys on the injection
lexicon" mechanism was never tested; distribution-shift miscalibration is an equally-supported
alternative. → §4(ii) downgraded to a "plausible contributing hypothesis."

Independently reproduced (not just V4's word): NotInject 38.5% / generic test-neg 21.4% / excess +17.2pp.

## Power & robustness caveats (added)

- **injecagent (V1/V2/V3/V5):** genuinely *uninformative* — 17 negatives, perfect separation, zero-width
  CI; **Gx negative at ALL three rungs** (tfidf −0.036, frozen −0.034, lora −0.014) → there was **no wall
  at any capacity** (val ≈ test throughout). FALSIFIED is mechanically correct but should read
  "uninformative / no-power," not "transfer." The `ci_low ≤ 0` rule conflates the two — a documented,
  pre-registered limitation (criteria.md:166), not a bug.
- **bipia (V2/V3):** lower-power — 468 negatives over only **3 negative clusters** → coarse bootstrap CI,
  but it is *wide* (conservative) and far from 0, so SURVIVES is unaffected.
- **browsesafe (V1/V2):** val is near-saturated (~0.9998) but test_roc is genuinely mid-range (0.555,
  std 0.0027) → the wall is real resolution, not a val artifact.
